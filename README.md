# Carriage Health

**After pregnancy loss, you deserve answers.**

More than 1 million miscarriages happen in the United States every year ([Health Affairs, 2024](https://www.healthaffairs.org/doi/10.1377/hlthaff.2023.00982)), and an estimated 23 million worldwide. If even 15% of those women found answers sooner, that would be over 150,000 women a year in the US alone with real information about their next pregnancy, before another loss.

Carriage Health is being built to help women access fertility and pregnancy-loss lab testing sooner, understand their results, and take informed next steps with their doctors.

## Why I'm building this

I'm Susie, the founder. Over six months, I had two missed miscarriages: losses with no warning signs, discovered on an ultrasound.

After the second, I wanted to understand why, and whether anything could be done before trying again. As an engineer and operator, I'm used to finding the root cause of a problem. Instead, I learned that a fuller evaluation often isn't available or covered by insurance until after two or three losses. The plan, in effect, was to wait and see if it happened again.

I believe in preventative care. "It's just bad luck" doesn't answer the questions women are left carrying, and no one should have to experience repeated loss to have those questions taken seriously.

## What we're building

Think "Function Health for fertility labs" *(a positioning analogy, not an affiliation)*:

1. **Access testing:** order relevant fertility and pregnancy-loss lab tests through a simple consumer experience.
2. **Understand results:** see results in a clear dashboard, with biomarkers explained in plain language.
3. **Plan next steps:** organize your results and share them with your care team.

Testing can't explain every loss or guarantee an outcome. The goal is earlier access to useful information, explored with qualified clinicians.

## How it's built

Built hands-on with **Claude Code** in VS Code, using AI-assisted development to take a regulated healthcare product from concept to working software.

**Stack:** Framer (marketing site) · TypeScript · Next.js · Supabase · Railway · Junction · Stripe. Designed with HIPAA requirements in mind, with BAA-covered vendors wherever health data is handled.
